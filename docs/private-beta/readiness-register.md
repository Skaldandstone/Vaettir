## Scope and behavior

Three enrolled external organizations, five full seats and two read-only seats each, 500 credits per UTC calendar month. Free, no automatic overage, no beta self-service tier changes. Unused credits, including staff top-ups, expire at the next monthly reset. Staff adjustments append history and cannot overdraw. An unused enrollment reserves a cohort slot until staff revoke it; suspension/deletion does not silently authorize a replacement team.

Pending, unexpired invitations reserve seats. Existing service accounts use full memberships and therefore consume full seats. Revocation immediately disables the credential. Normally it also releases the seat; a legacy service account that is the sole owner retains its membership until staff transfer ownership to a human and retry cleanup. The revocation receipt explicitly reports this pending cleanup without leaving the key usable. Reserve one of the five seats for CI when needed. Any decision to separate service-account quotas requires a follow-up product change, not a hidden bypass.

Shared framework/control/plan-type catalogs are global in the current schema. Staff manage their definitions during beta. Team-specific data must not be placed in those catalogs; existing definitions need review before external admission. Tenant mapping, evidence and sign-off remain project-scoped.

Android is the primary companion. iOS compilation and physical-device acceptance are separate gates. Checkout, paid pricing, push, full mobile parity, enterprise SSO, vendor migrations and UI-framework refactors remain deferred.
